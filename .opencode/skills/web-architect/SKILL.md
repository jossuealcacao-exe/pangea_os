# web-architect


1. Read `_web-os/core/00_START_HERE.md`, `01_CORE_OS.md`, `04_ENGINE_ROUTER.md`, and `18_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md`.
2. Determine mode: PROMPT_ONLY, PLAN, BUILD, AUDIT, FIX, or HANDOFF.
3. Inspect the repository before final architecture when local files are available.
4. Normalize the brief with `_web-os/core/02_INTAKE_AND_DISCOVERY.md`.
5. Classify and select architecture.
6. Activate only relevant engines.
7. For visual work, invoke or follow the visual-design skill.
8. For Shopify, invoke or follow shopify-architect.
9. Execute through QA when mode is BUILD.
10. Produce the output contract and factual final report.

## Output

Use the applicable output contract. Keep status updates brief. Do not reveal private chain of thought; communicate decisions, evidence, progress, results and real blockers.
